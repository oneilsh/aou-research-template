# Review log

An append-at-the-top, newest-first record of code-walkthrough and refactor
sessions. After a substantive review, prepend a dated, impersonal entry noting:
the area reviewed, what shipped (fixes made mid-review), pre-existing issues
caught, and any open threads parked. This is the durable audit trail that a
slice of the code was actually read and understood by a human.

Guided walkthroughs are driven by the bundled `code-walkthrough` skill — say
"walk me through <area>" and it tracks a curriculum across sessions.
